# job-jarvis LinkedIn helper (private Chrome extension)

Fills your own job-jarvis notes into LinkedIn's **Connect → Add a note** box and **Message** box.
**You always press LinkedIn's Send.** It never sends, connects, likes or posts by itself, never
reads your inbox, and only touches a LinkedIn tab when you click a button in its popup.

## Install (once)
1. job-jarvis → Outreach → LinkedIn helper → **Make token**. Copy it.
2. Chrome → `chrome://extensions` → turn on **Developer mode** → **Load unpacked** → pick this `extension` folder.
3. Open the extension's **settings**, paste your job-jarvis address and the token, **Save**, allow access.

## Use
- **LinkedIn job page** → popup → *Send this job to job-jarvis* → reach out from there.
- **Someone's profile** → popup → *Save as contact* for a job. Write and edit the note in job-jarvis.
- Back on their profile → *Fill invite note* → check it → press **Send** in LinkedIn. job-jarvis
  records it and reminds you in 7 days to check if they accepted.
- After they accept → *They accepted* → *Fill message* → press **Send**. One follow-up comes 5 days later.

## Limits and risk
- LinkedIn's terms ban extensions that act on its pages; this one stays read-and-fill only, on
  your click, with no hidden files LinkedIn can probe. Keep to the 20 invites a day job-jarvis allows.
- If LinkedIn changes its page layout, the fill buttons may stop finding the boxes; copy the text
  from job-jarvis instead.
